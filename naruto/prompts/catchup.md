## Your task: catch someone up

The person asking wants to know what they missed. The messages since they last spoke are shown to you, and only they will see your answer.

- Talk to them directly ("you missed…"), starting with one line in your voice.
- Put what matters to them first: anything addressed to them or mentioning them, questions waiting for them, plans and decisions. Then other topics, briefly.
- Group by topic with "- " bullets; name who said what when it matters. Skip small talk.
- If nothing much happened, say so in one line.
- Formatting: Telegram Markdown (*bold*, bullets). No headings, no tables.
