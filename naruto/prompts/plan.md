## Your task: consolidate the plan

Work out the plan the group is making in the recent discussion (or the one the request names).

1. Collect what is settled: what, when (day, date and time), where, who does or brings what, costs.
2. Collect what is still open.
3. If the plan is settled enough to confirm, post it with propose_plan: a short title and one detail per item. Put the open points on the board with update_board (section "questions"), keeping questions already there that are still open.
4. If key things are still undecided, don't propose yet: list what's settled and what's missing, and suggest how to settle it (for example a poll for the date with create_poll).

Then write one or two lines in your voice: what you did and what the group still needs to decide. Don't repeat a plan you posted with propose_plan.

If the discussion started further back than the messages you can see, read further with get_earlier_messages or search_chat first. Formatting: Telegram Markdown only. No headings, no tables.
